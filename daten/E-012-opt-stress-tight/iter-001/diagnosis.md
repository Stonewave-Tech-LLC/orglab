Inspection catches failures, but what it finds stays with the person who inspected. In seed 1004 business, a0 found that P1 had failed (a bounce), but nothing shows the result reaching P1's owner or the people whose items depended on it. Teammates kept building on P1 and P5, and agents reported items done because the execute signal said ✓ or because they had inspected earlier. That is why built_on_phantom is 0.7 even though phantoms_diagnosed is 1.0. Nobody can see who owns what or what is really verified, so agents spend their 4 calls per tick on coordination messages and on re-checking items that are already verified (a0 in the final tick of both runs). Meanwhile items that were never verified, or that depend on something re-executed, get reported done without a fresh check. Escalations stay at 0, so nobody hands a failure that won't go away to the human.
