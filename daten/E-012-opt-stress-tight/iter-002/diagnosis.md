The work does not fail through false reporting. Phantoms are diagnosed in every run and false_done is low. It fails through time running out, because the scarce budget of 4 calls per tick goes to duplicated effort and coordination churn. In seed 2002, a0 spent two whole ticks inspecting a3's items before they had been executed. a0 also claimed dependents it could not yet run, while a3 sat idle and said 'I'm free', so the last unblocked items only ran at tick 8. In seed 2004, up to four agents inspected the same item (P8, P4), and several agents re-inspected items their owners had already verified. As a result, the bounce on the root item P8 was caught by a non-owner only at tick 8, and its downstream items were squeezed into the final tick. Every status update was broadcast to all three peers, even when only one of them needed it. There is no shared rule on who checks what or who holds what, so verification is both duplicated and late. The earlier board and verification norms did not help because they added more checking instead of assigning it.
