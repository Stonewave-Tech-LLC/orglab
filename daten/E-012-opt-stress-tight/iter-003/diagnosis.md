Two problems account for most of the losses. First, when an item failed, its owner did not reliably fix it. In seed 3000, P1 failed its check in tick 2. Three teammates then inspected it again in ticks 2–3 and asked a3 to fix it, but nobody took it over, and items that depended on it (such as P5) were later reported done while not working. Second, agents spent their four actions per tick inspecting other people's items that hadn't been run yet ('not processed yet' over and over). With so few actions, that meant fewer checks on their own work and on the items their work depended on. 'Done' was based on a check made earlier, not one made at the moment of reporting, so a broken dependency did not stop the report. Earlier attempts to fix this failed in two ways: allowing only the owner to check made things significantly worse, and adding a shared board did not help. So the fix should be clearer responsibility for repairs and a final check before reporting done, not less checking overall.
