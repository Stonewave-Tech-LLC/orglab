The team runs out of tool calls before it runs out of work, and messaging is what uses them up. Each agent has 4 calls per tick, and broadcasting status to all three teammates costs 3 of them. In tick 1, a1 spent 3 of 4 calls on one broadcast. In tick 8, a0 spent 3 calls telling everyone things they already knew, while P4 was still broken. Calls also go to duplicate checks: in the final tick, a3 re-inspected four items that teammates had verified moments earlier, then found P4's regression with no calls left to fix it. Failures are passed along instead of acted on. a0 found P8's failure and messaged a3 rather than re-executing it, so fixes slip a tick or more. The result is that late-surfacing problems remain at END: phantom successes, and earlier items broken by later rollouts (P4 broken by P9, P2 overwritten by P12).
