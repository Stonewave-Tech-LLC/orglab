The team's main bottleneck is exclusive ownership combined with slow owners. Agents claim long future chains in tick 1. Once an item fails or is reversed, only the owner can re-execute it. Everyone else then spends scarce actions inspecting the same item and broadcasting the same pleas, while dependents wait (P1 in seed 3003 blocked P2, P3, P7 and P4 until the last tick). Broadcasting to all three peers costs three of the four tool calls per tick, so coordination crowds out execution and repair. 'Done' is reported once and never revisited, so items reversed by later work (P4 by P16, P1 by P12) stay reported done. No one owns the final state of the whole set, so these false-done items and some unprocessed items (P13, P14) survive to the end of the run.
