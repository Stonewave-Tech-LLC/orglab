Attention, not capability, is the bottleneck, and the mesh spends it badly. In tick 1 the agents broadcast competing plans to all three peers, using most of their four calls. Then the agents who were waiting spent call after call inspecting other agents' root items (P1, P2, P3, P4: 'not processed yet') instead of being told when those items were ready. Meanwhile the agents who owned those roots claimed them and did not execute them in the same tick. Ownership was split across dependency lines, so most agents sat blocked on someone else and asked for status, and repairs (P8, P13, P7, P10) were pushed into the last one or two ticks. Done was reported as soon as an item was verified, even though later writes by other owners (e.g. P11 overwrote P8) could still reverse it. Nobody re-checked after the last write, which leaves about two false-dones per run.
